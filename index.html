01 · The premium tier

STALK

A parent who spends forty dollars on their child's shampoo walks into the vitamin aisle and finds almost nothing built for them. Not because parents will not pay. Because the category has not asked them to.
The gap

The premium end of the children's aisle is thin.

Look at children's supplements and it is mostly gummies, sugar and cartoon characters, clustered at the same low price. The premium options that exist sell reassurance or clean labels; none of them is built around development, in the one category where the buyer is a parent deciding for their own child.

The demand for something better is already proven, and it is all going somewhere else. NuBest, Doctor Taller and TrueHeight sell growth and development products at multiples of the shelf price, direct to consumer, on worry. None of them is on a shelf you control.

What that costs today. The highest-intent shopper in the aisle, the one who would happily pay three times the average, is either buying nothing or buying it online from a brand you cannot stock.

A comment from a pediatrician or a comparison in a playground triggers the same question every time. Is my child okay, and am I doing enough?

Every brand in the aisle answers that with worry. STALK answers it with evidence, and makes the daily minute feel like a decision a parent made rather than a fear they gave in to. One serving a day, in whichever format suits the household, across three age bands so a family stays in the brand for a decade rather than a season.

02 · The system play

PLUS 1

Every hydration brand on your shelf sells flavours. This one sells a week. The shopper leaves with three sticks instead of one, because they are not choosing a taste, they are choosing occasions.
The gap

The fastest-growing category, and every brand sells it the same way.

Hydration is growing faster than almost anything else you stock, and every brand in it is built the same way: one formula, four flavours, one occasion. The shopper picks a flavour, buys one box, and the repeat is whatever the flavour fatigue allows.

The cult brand sits at $1.50 a stick and private label programmes land near $0.50. The whole set is competing on price inside a single occasion.

The open position is the one above flavour: sell by occasion instead. Morning is a different purchase from Workout, which is a different purchase from Travel, and a person who buys the logic buys several.

Price points per the Wegmans category analysis. Market context: $8B+ globally, growing around 9% a year through 2030, with category growth running +52% year on year and +29% ahead of sports drinks. Grand View Research 2024; SPINS.

03 · The missing format

Grab-and-go protein

Protein has never been built for the checkout, the desk drawer or the ten-second pack. The single-serve sachet exists, but only as a trial size. Nobody has made it the product.
The gap

The biggest macro in the store, sold only in formats that need a kitchen.

Count the protein formats you stock. Tubs, bars, ready-to-drink bottles. Every one of them either needs a scoop and a shaker, or is a chilled bottle carrying water around the country.

Single-serve protein powder exists only as the sample at the bottom of a tub brand's range. Nothing is built for the checkout, the travel section, or the person who did not come in for it.

The zone this opens. A flat sachet is the only protein format that can sit at checkout, in a travel bay, in a hotel channel, and in a multipack beside the tubs. It is also the first way a shopper can compare protein by the serving.

04 · The missing shopper

Latin flavour
functional powders

Your snack aisle is full of chamoy, tajin and mango con chile. Your functional aisle is berry, citrus and blue raspberry. Same shopper, same trip, two completely different assumptions about what they like.
The gap

The palate that already runs one aisle is missing from the other.

Latin flavour stopped being regional some time ago. It is the default palate of shoppers under thirty, of every background, and your snack, candy and beverage sets already reflect that. The functional set has not moved.

Taste Salud saw this first and proved it: agua fresca flavours in a functional powder, growing fast. But it is one young brand, one category deep, on rented manufacturing, and the majors still have not moved. The window is open, not empty.

Why we can win it. Flavour is the whole product here, and flavour is the part of the supply chain Taste Salud has to rent. We hold 1,000+ in rotation and run a flavour lab on site, so one flavour system can carry hydration, collagen, protein and whatever comes next. A co-packed brand has to renegotiate for every one of those.
